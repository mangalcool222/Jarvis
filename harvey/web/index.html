Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a self-serve software tool or a Done-For-You (DFY) service?
TrackKaro Outbound OS is delivered as an autonomous, fully managed system. We handle secondary domain infrastructure, mailbox warmup, intent scraping, AI prompt tuning, and CRM delivery. Your team simply attends qualified sales meetings booked on your calendar.
How is our primary domain protected from spam flags?
We never send cold outbound from your primary corporate root domain. We configure dedicated secondary mirror domains with full SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment, pre-warmed for 21 days before any outbound volume is released.
What deal sizes and industries are best suited?
High-ticket B2B service agencies, SaaS platforms, high-end commercial clinics, and luxury real estate with client lifetime values between $2,000 and $50,000+.
How quickly can our outbound pipeline go live?
Once your target ICP and intent signals are calibrated during our consultation, initial sample verified accounts are delivered within 48 hours, and automated outreach begins immediately following mailbox warmup verification.
